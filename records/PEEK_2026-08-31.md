# Dry run on partial Hong Kong data — recorded peek

**Date** 2026-08-31. **Coverage** 12,120 hourly files with data, 2021-02-25 to 2024-06-26 — roughly
60% of the acquisition window and none of 2024H2, 2025 or 2026.

**This is not the registered result and will not be reported as one.** The registered test runs on the
full window under `PREREG_external_test_v0.3_amendment.md` §8.

## Why it was run

To exercise the frozen pipeline end to end on real data before its single real execution. It found one
fault immediately: `R["df"]` did not exist in the shared collapse routine, which uses `df_band` and
`df_theta`. The fault was in a print statement and affected no computed quantity. Fixed, recorded in
the script header, and the script re-stamped.

## Why looking is not a breach

The protection a pre-registration provides is not that the author never looked; it is that the
specification cannot change after looking. Both the specification
(`e03a2407f90856d2381af91b2e5f8c956e6e47db7e67422bd25fd26d44a1eb89`) and the analysis code were hashed
and anchored before any Hong Kong result existed. Neither can be altered silently. This document
records the peek so that the sequence is legible rather than inferred.

## What the dry run showed

| level | legs | H1 | H2 | H3 crossing | β_C |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A, near-24h | 498,211 | pass | **fail** | 0.211 | −1.505 |
| B, HK day session | 346,626 | pass | **fail** | 0.268 | −1.533 |
| C, sub-sessions | 341,926 | pass | **fail** | 0.226 | −1.411 |

**H1 passes everywhere.** Band means fall monotonically at all three levels, F(5,·) between 34 and 117.

**H3 passes everywhere.** The unity crossing lands at 0.21, 0.27 and 0.23 against a VN30F1M value near
0.20 and a registered window of [0.15, 0.30].

**H2 fails, but only on its significance condition.** p_θ is 0.013, 0.017 and 0.000. All three
magnitude criteria pass at every level: SS_θ/SS_band is 0.047, 0.132, 0.077 against a 0.35 threshold,
and within-over-between is 0.208, 0.308, 0.202 against 0.40. With roughly twice VN30F1M's leg count the
cell means are estimated more precisely and small threshold effects become detectable. The magnitude
criteria were introduced into v0.1 §5.1 for exactly this reason, and they hold.

**The §4 ordering fails.** Predicted β_C(sub) < β_C(day) < β_C(24h); observed −1.411, −1.533, −1.505.
The sub-session level is the shallowest, not the deepest, and the three values sit within 0.12 of each
other.

## The uncomfortable reading, stated now rather than later

A near-24-hour window whose midnight boundary binds nothing produces essentially the same compression
as a real trading session. If that survives the full window, the finite-available-price-space account
does not gain support from this ladder, and the component of the relation that is a statistical
consequence of normalising by a range that contains the measured leg becomes the more economical
explanation of what the ladder sees.

Against that: the leave-one-leg-out test on VN30F1M removed the self-inclusion path directly and the
relation survived, retaining 84–87% of its rank association within window, with threshold invariance
measuring cleaner rather than worse. Self-inclusion is therefore not the whole story on the discovery
market. Whether it is the larger part of what the Hong Kong ladder measures is a different question,
and this dry run cannot settle it.

No window, threshold, bin or criterion is revised on the basis of anything above. v0.3 §4 already
states the consequence of a failed ordering: it weakens the mechanistic interpretation and does not
invalidate the VN30F1M relation.

## Superseded, 2026-08-31

The Level A figures above were computed under an implementation that grouped by Hong Kong calendar
date. The instrument's tradable window crosses midnight, so that grouping reset the directional-change
process inside a trading window and merged parts of two different windows. **β_C(A) = −1.505 is an
invalid implementation and is not interpretable as the pre-registered Level A.**

It is retained here rather than deleted. The correction is recorded in
`PREREG_external_test_v0.5_amendment.md` (`8805f895…`), frozen before any corrected Level-A statistic
existed. Levels B and C are unaffected by the defect.

## Second interim inspection, 2026-08-31, corrected implementation

Same sample as above — everything through 2024-06-26 — re-run under the Level A correction registered
in `PREREG_external_test_v0.5_amendment.md` (`8805f895…`). **No new data was exposed**: this is the
portion already disclosed as seen. The held-back window was refused by an explicit `--seen-only` guard
built into the pipeline for this purpose.

| level | β_C, calendar grouping (invalid) | β_C, corrected | H1 | H3 crossing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A, full tradable window | −1.505 | **−1.385** | pass | 0.225 |
| B, HK day session | −1.533 | −1.503 | pass | 0.271 |
| C, sub-session | −1.411 | −1.404 | pass | 0.227 |

The correction moved Level A by 0.12 and left B and C almost untouched, as expected: only A was
mis-grouped.

**H1 and H3 pass at every level under the corrected implementation.** The unity crossing sits at 0.225,
0.271 and 0.227 against a VN30F1M value near 0.20 and a registered window of [0.15, 0.30].

**H2 continues to fail on its significance condition alone** — p_θ = 0.007, 0.008, 0.000 — while all
three magnitude criteria pass at every level. This is reported under v0.4 §4 as *conditioning on δ/R
absorbs most, but not all, of the threshold dependence*.

**The §4 ordering still fails, but not flatly.** It is now half-right and informative:

    A_full  −1.385   widest window, shallowest slope   ← as predicted
    B_day   −1.503   steepest
    C_sub   −1.404   narrowest window, but shallower than the day session   ← against prediction

Compression deepens from the full window to the day session and then reverses at the sub-session. The
non-monotonicity is a more specific result than a flat gradient, and v0.3 §7 named a candidate for it
in advance: narrowing the window also raises the frequency of directional-change resets and truncates
more events at the boundary. At the sub-session level those effects are strongest.

Nothing above licenses a change to any criterion. The held-back test remains the only clean read.
